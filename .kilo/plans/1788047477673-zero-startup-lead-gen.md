# Zero Startup Local Services Business Plan: Lead Generation for Trades

**Business:** Local Lead Generation for HVAC, Plumbing & Electrical Contractors  
**Startup Capital Required:** $0  
**Target Market:** Residential homeowners seeking emergency and installation services in suburban Southeast U.S.  
**Service Model:** Generate qualified leads for local trades using free digital channels; sell leads to contractors  
**Revenue Target:** $1,000,000 Year 2–3

### Phased Launch Strategy

| Phase | Timeline | Revenue Target | Key Activities |
|-------|----------|----------------|----------------|
| **Phase 1: Asset Building** | Months 1–3 | $0–$500 | Build 3–5 Google Business Profiles for "virtual" service companies in target cities, optimize for local SEO, start generating organic leads |

| **Phase 3: Scale** | Months 10–24 | $10,000–$42,000/month | Expand to 10+ cities, add 15–20 contractor clients, implement automation tools, reach $1M annualized run rate |

### Revenue Models

#### Model 1: Pay-Per-Lead (PPL)
- Price: $25–$150 per qualified lead depending on trade and job value
- HVAC: $40–$75 per lead
- Plumbing: $35–$60 per lead
- Electrical: $30–$50 per lead
- Roofing: $80–$120 per lead
- Contractor pays only for leads that meet criteria (e.g., phone call >90 seconds, valid address, service needed)

#### Model 2: Flat Monthly Retainer
- Price: $1,000–$3,000/month per exclusive territory
- Includes: 10–30 qualified leads/month, call tracking, CRM integration
- Best for: Contractors with consistent volume who want predictable costs

#### Model 3: Hybrid (Recommended)
- Base retainer: $1,000–$1,500/month
- Plus: $30–$75 per lead above quota
- Gives contractor predictability, gives you upside on volume

**Total monthly tool cost: $0–$100/month**

#### Hiring Roadmap (All Optional Until Revenue Proof)
| Role | Hire When | Cost |
|------|-----------|------|
| Solo operator | Month 0 | $0 (your time) |
| VA for call answering/scheduling | 5+ clients, $5K+/month revenue | $4–$8/hour (OnlineJobs.ph) |
| Content writer | 10+ sites needed | $50–$200 per article |
| Sales assistant | 15+ active leads | $500–$1,000/month |

#### Fulfillment Model
1. **Lead Capture:** Homeowner fills form or calls your GBP/landing page
2. **Qualification:** AI chatbot or simple script collects name, address, service needed, timeline
3. **Routing:** Lead forwarded to assigned contractor via SMS/email/CRM within 5 minutes
4. **Tracking:** Call tracking records all interactions for proof of delivery
5. **Billing:** Automatic invoicing via Stripe based on qualified leads delivered

## Summary: The Path to $1,000,000

**This is a true zero-startup business.** You need nothing more than a laptop, phone, and internet connection.

1. **Months 1–3:** Build 3–5 Google Business Profiles and simple websites in one target city. Optimize for local SEO. Start generating organic leads from free search traffic. Zero cost, time investment only.

2. **Months 4–9:** Approach local contractors with proof of leads. Sell first 3–5 clients at $25–$50/lead. Prove conversion quality. Reinvest early revenue into more sites/GBPs.

3. **Months 10–24:** Scale to 25–40 contractor clients across 10–15 cities. Mix pay-per-lead ($40–$120/lead) with flat retainers ($1,500–$3,000/month). Hire part-time VA. Hit $1,000,000 annualized run rate by Month 24.

**Unit economics at scale:**
- 35 contractor clients × $2,400/month average = $84,000/month = $1,008,000/year
- 92% gross margin ($73,000/month profit)
- Minimal overhead: $2,000–$6,000/month for tools, VA, domains
- **Net profit: $65,000–$70,000/month at $1M revenue**

**The business is asset-based.** Each ranked website/GBP is a digital asset worth 20–40x monthly profit. A site generating $2,000/month is worth $40,000–$80,000 on the market. Your portfolio of 30+ sites could be worth $1M+ as a saleable asset.

**Competitive advantage:** You own the lead generation assets. Contractors own the customer relationship. No inventory, no employees required, no licensing. The barrier to entry is knowing how to rank local websites and manage contractor relationships.
